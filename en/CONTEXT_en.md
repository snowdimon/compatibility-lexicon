## Interdisciplinary Context: Compatibility Lexicon

### Cybernetics & Systems Theory
The problem of inter-system interaction and ontological conflict: how can systems with different internal logic and values communicate without mutual destruction? The Lexicon offers not unification, but the creation of a mediator interface that preserves the identity of each system.

### Philosophy
The problem of the Other (E. Levinas, J.-P. Sartre) and the conflict of values in a multicultural world. The principle asserts that interfacing is possible without absorption or isolation, through respect for red lines and honest dialogue.

### Psychology
Conflict studies, family therapy, communication problems. The Compatibility Lexicon is a protocol for healthy conflict: defining the inalienable (red lines), a mediation procedure (mediator language), a dignified exit from a relationship (separation protocol) without total war.
